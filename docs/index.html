TL;DR. Each token's loss falls in one sigmoid-shaped step at its own learning time \(\tau\), and the steps share one pulse shape. The smooth scaling law is therefore set by when tokens are learned, the learning-time spectrum \(p(\tau)\), not by how. Reshaping the training data by learning time makes validation loss fall 11% faster.
